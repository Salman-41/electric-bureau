## Research outcome

- **Stereoscope:** History and object records are verified. A downloadable, object-specific Smithsonian 3D scan is available for a French A. Mattey stereoscope dated 1905–1920, not an 1859 Holmes viewer. CC0 applies to the listed scan and images. Use it only with its correct date and identity.
- **Zoetrope:** Smithsonian records document period instruments and strips; Oxford and peer-reviewed research support the timeline. A specific open image is available from Cooper Hewitt. No object-specific downloadable 3D scan was verified.
- **Tinfoil phonograph:** The Smithsonian Edison object and Library of Congress history establish the invention and mechanism. Smithsonian media for object NMAH 855475 is marked CC0. No object-specific downloadable 3D model was verified.
- **Pneumatic postal system:** National Postal Museum records document the canister and system; LOC has a period New York Post Office tube photograph. LOC records warn users to assess rights; this photo is not cleared for reuse from the record alone. No object-specific downloadable 3D model was verified.

CC0 does not clear trademarks, privacy, or other third-party rights. Smithsonian attribution is encouraged, though not required for CC0.
