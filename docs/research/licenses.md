## Institution policy links

- Smithsonian [Terms of Use](https://www.si.edu/termsofuse): only material with a CC0 icon is under CC0; other usage-condition content may require permission. CC0 does not guarantee freedom from non-copyright rights.
- Smithsonian [Open Access FAQ](https://www.si.edu/openaccess/faq): CC0 does not require attribution, but credit plus link is encouraged; users must not imply Smithsonian endorsement.
- Smithsonian [NMAH Rights and Reproductions](https://americanhistory.si.edu/collections/about/rights-and-reproductions): specific guidance on Open Access assets and permissions for other media.
- Met [Image and Data Resources](https://www.metmuseum.org/policies/image-resources): CC0 is for images of works the museum believes are in the public domain; verify per object.
- Met [Collection API](https://metmuseum.github.io/): `isPublicDomain`, `primaryImage`, and `primaryImageSmall` are the fields to validate for an image.
- LOC [Prints & Photographs rights and restrictions](https://guides.loc.gov/p-and-p-rights-and-restrictions/rights) and [risk assessment](https://guides.loc.gov/p-and-p-rights-and-restrictions/risk-assessment): the user is responsible for rights clearance; LOC does not generally own collection copyrights.
